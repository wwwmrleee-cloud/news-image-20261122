One 1024x1024 image summing up the world news of Sunday 22 November 2026 in **48 sourced stories across 7 regions**, every story marked with its source and a confidence level. Lead stories: COP31 climate talks in overtime in Antalya with fossil fuels dropped from the draft (EU: good deal or no deal); FBI top-ten cartel kingpin Chapo Isidro captured in a Mexico army raid; GTA VI's $3.79bn day-one sales, the biggest entertainment launch ever. Also inside: the FAA's 4+ hour nationwide ground stop, the Microsoft 365 outage restored, Denmark accusing Russia of a plot to kill rapper Kyivstoner, Estonia shutting its road borders with Russia, Poland reporting Russian missiles on its soil, Russia's 400-drone barrage on Ukraine, Modi opening the 9.76km Nitish Setu bridge, Cyclone Akvan moving 370,000 people to safety, the Peru M6.9 quake, and a UTC timeline, a date check (Sunday 22 November 2026, day 326 of 2026) and a numbers band.

Regions: North America 7, Latin America 5, Europe 10, Africa 5, Middle East 6, Asia-Pacific 9, Global 6.

Confidence rule: HIGH means the source is a wire service or a major outlet (AP, AFP, Reuters, NYT, CNN, WSJ, BBC); MED means any other outlet. Source labels used on the page: AP, AFP, REUTERS, NYT, CNN, WSJ, BBC, NYPOST, ZEE, SBS, NATION, IEC, NASA, BERKELEY (Berkeley Earth), SPIEGEL, NWS, JAGRAN, BIZBUZZ.

A pure Python standard-library program draws it with a hand-typed 5x7 pixel font and its own PNG encoder: no fonts, no images, no network, no clock, no chance, so two runs write byte-identical files.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```
